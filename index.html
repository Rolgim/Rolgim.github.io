What the four sites share

  • Everything is sourced

    Every figure on a fact sheet links to the page it comes from: the space agency, the mission team or the archive.

  • Approximations are flagged

    When a drawing, an orbit or an orientation is schematic rather than measured, the site says so next to it.

  • Real data

    The images come from the public archives of NASA, ESA, JAXA and their partners, and the 3D models from openly licensed libraries, each with its own credit line.

  • Two languages

    Every page exists in English and in French, and the language button keeps you on the same page.

  • Accessible

    Tested against WCAG 2.1 level AA in a real browser: keyboard navigation, contrast, text alternatives for the 3D views.

  • Open and non-commercial

    The code is under the Apache 2.0 licence. Each image keeps its own licence, listed in each site's credits register.
